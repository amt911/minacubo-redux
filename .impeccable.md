## Design Context

> Starter brief for the impeccable skills. Note: this is a **Three.js voxel game** — most of the "design" is the 3D world, which is out of impeccable's scope. Impeccable applies to the **DOM chrome**: the lil-gui HUD, menus, and any overlays.

### Users
Players (and the maintainer) messing with a browser-based Minecraft-style voxel sandbox — placing/removing blocks, wandering procedurally generated terrain, watching the day/night cycle. They want the world to be the focus and the controls to stay out of the way.

### Brand Personality
**Playful · Blocky · Unfussy.** A hobby voxel sandbox — honest and fun, not corporate. The chrome should feel light and get out of the way of the world.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **The 3D world is the product.** Blocky, pixel-textured, warm daylight fading to night. This lives in Three.js, not in CSS.
- **Chrome is minimal**: the lil-gui control panel, an optional HUD/crosshair, and simple menus. Keep them unobtrusive, legible over any scene, and visually consistent.
- **Typography**: one legible UI font (optionally a pixel/mono accent to echo the voxel theme) for menus and HUD.
- **Not**: heavy UI framing that competes with the canvas, or decorative overlays that block the view.

### Design Principles
1. **The world first.** Overlays stay minimal and never fight the 3D scene.
2. **Readable over any scene.** HUD/menu text keeps contrast against bright sky and dark night alike.
3. **Consistent, quiet chrome.** lil-gui and menus share one calm visual language.
4. **Center-screen focus.** Interaction (crosshair/raycast) is centered; controls sit at the edges.
